## Signature Mechanic Canvas

Verb: fall home (climb fast, then fall carefully — the descent is the skill, not the climb).
Teach beat: first descent drop is short, no hazards, land on a wide harbor deck.
Press beat: descent drops lengthen under exhaust drip timing and a satellite crossing.
Twist: final descent onto the droneship crown deck while its roller guard patrols the landing zone.
Tell: exhaust drip flash before each fall line; roller spin on the crown deck; altitude milestone plates at checkpoints.
