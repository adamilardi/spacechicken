Facts (real): Four astronauts aboard Orion (crew-named Integrity) launched from Kennedy Space Center on April 1, 2026, flew a free-return-style loop around the Moon on April 6 (far side, with a solar eclipse seen from deep space), reached 252,756 miles from Earth — the farthest humans have travelled — and splashed down off San Diego on April 10, 2026 after 695,081 miles in about 10 days. Re-entry ran near 25,000 mph with the heat shield near 5,000F, then slowed to about 20 mph under parachutes for splashdown; post-flight inspection found 9 char-loss spots versus 103 on Artemis I after a changed re-entry trajectory.

## Signature Mechanic Canvas

Verb: drop-bonk (commit to the fall; stomp the Integrity Sprite on a crater floor to bounce up the far wall).
Classification: mandatory. Signature proof: an honest crown run records bonk contacts on the Integrity Sprites (bonk count >= 3 across the three crater floors) before the well; bypass test — a run that never bonks (walks/jumps only, avoiding every sprite) cannot leave the first crater (the rim deck sits ~460px above the crater floor, past the double-jump rise at gravity 240, and the floor has no exit at route height), so the bypass fails in the crater instead of reaching the crown.
Teach beat: first crater is hazard-free past the sink; the sprite patrols the gap off its floor and the bounce lands on a wide rim deck; missing the sprite drops to the crater floor deck beside it, survivable, and the player retries the fall.
Press beat: second crater sits under the far-side particle streams and the arc-throat laser, so the fall must be timed as well as aimed; third crater on the return sits under plasma drip timing.
Twist: the crown is not bounced to at all — after the last rise the route falls the splashdown well on foot between the heat beams, so the falling verb ends as a descent, not a bounce.
Tell: sprite heat-shield glow pulses and the bonk chevron/marker rides above it; drip flash before each plasma line; beam warning before each heat gate.
