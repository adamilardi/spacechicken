# Level 27 — Cold Chain Down (news-to-level)

Story: "Europe on alert after supermarket freezers hit by cyber-attacks in 10 countries" — BBC, Nov 3 2026, 13:12 GMT. https://www.bbc.co.uk/news/articles/cwy8lm1m59mvo

Facts (real): On Monday Nov 2 2026 hackers hit supermarket refrigeration chains in at least 10 European countries (Belgium, Estonia, Finland, Ireland, Italy, Luxembourg, Netherlands, Portugal, Germany, Sweden); freezer temperatures soared as high as 50C (122F), forcing retailers to discard food and leaving shelves empty. Italy's agency blamed obsolete security systems with interfaces directly exposed to the internet and inadequate authentication; Portugal's Intermarché and Germany's Rewe traced incidents to external service providers. Belgium was hardest-hit (dozens of stores, Delhaize); Ireland's SuperValu replenishment was already underway. The EU warned of a deliberate attempt to disrupt the food supply chain, three weeks after an attack disrupted ~700 French stores with losses in the tens of millions of euros. No-one admitted the attacks; the UK NCSC warned retailers to update fridge-control software.

## Signature Mechanic Canvas

Verb: ride the cold phase — route between vent refuges on the global flip countdown, crossing each coil gate inside its cold window, choosing the low tunnel or the high ridge at the split.
Classification: mandatory. Signature proof: the trunk gates are full-lock (gap beams span deck to ceiling, the band fills its 64px tunnel), so a bypass run that ignores phases and walks the gates dies in the coils; the high ridge skips the band at drone-and-drip cost instead. Observable is pilot phase-vs-death timing from capture telemetry (deaths cluster on hot-phase crossings, clears cross on cold). Bypass test: walk the trunk without waiting for a cold window — dies at gate 1.
Teach beat: aisle 1 has one gap-beam gate; the deck before it is the waiting refuge and the tell runs a full cycle before the player must commit.
Press beat: the split — low road walks the heat-band tunnel on cold, high road climbs the drone ridge under melt timing; both rejoin past the compactor's shadow. Aisle 3's gap gate sits past the ridge drone and twin melt lines, so the crossing must be phased and dodged at once.
Twist: the finale vault's coils are dead — no timing at all, just a clean grab past the patrolling guard under alternating melt lines.
Tell: the coils are the tell — the whole store slams hot/cold on one visible 2.8s clock, and a thermometer prop marks every gate; melt drips fall on fixed intervals beside the gates.
