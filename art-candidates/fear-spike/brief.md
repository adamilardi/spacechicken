# Brief: fear-spike (level 18, news-to-level)

Story: Nasdaq 100 third correction of 2026 on AI-spending doubts — VIX +23% to 21,
Intel/Reddit -29%. The level climbs the VIX fear spike while the crash falls.
Style anchor: skyhook night palette (#060b18/#101a33/#1d2c4d, cyan #7df9ff,
red #ff4a3c) plus the shipped level-17 generated set (flat cartoon side-view,
dark navy, cyan conduits, red chart accents).

## Place — three directions, one survivor

- level-fear-spike-a (SURVIVOR): gauge dome. Giant VIX dial, needle pinned red,
  exchange towers. Landmark: the dial. Set-piece silhouette: gauge arc.
- level-fear-spike-b (KILLED): needle tower. Too close to skyhook's tether
  landmark rhythm; would read as a skyhook palette variant.
- level-fear-spike-c (KILLED): candle storm. Tilted slabs risk unreadable
  solids; no single landmark.

Composition devices (vs skyhook catalog: ribbon+stars / gantry frames / pylon
landmark / pad slab): sky = gauge dial (new); far = blocky exchange towers
(new); mid = dial landmark. Two differences: sky, far. New device claimed for
the background: full-bleed generated fear-gauge plate over the skyhook render.

Platforms/props: reuse shipped canvas reads — skyhookDeck (main),
skyhookPanel (shelves), vaultDeck (gold bell perch at crown). Story props
(-29% crater plates, closing bell) are canvas paint in the build phase.
