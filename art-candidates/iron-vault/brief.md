# Brief: Iron Vault branch level (level 16)

A short sealed branch off the Crimson Womb: clearing L12 offers Rust Harbor
or the Iron Vault, and the vault rejoins at Ember Foundry. The vault reuses
the `bastion` interior background type (new paint would repeat the relay),
with its own deck and prop in the shipped flat canvas style. Side view,
gameplay size, smoothing off. No raster files, no labels in the paint.

- `vaultDeck` 96x24 SOLID: dark bronze plates with a pale amber walk edge
  and seal rivets. Warm body keeps it off gunmetal bastionDeck.
- `vaultSeal` 24x48 DECOR: seal post with a glowing sigil. Thin and tall;
  never a landing surface.
