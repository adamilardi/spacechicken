# Brief: contra arc part 2 (levels 13-15)

Three more run-and-gun harborside-to-orbit stops after the Crimson Womb, in the
shipped flat canvas style. Side view, horizontal scroll, four baked parallax
layers, `scrollFactorY = 1`. Distant paint stays quieter than the chicken,
hazards, and platform edges. No raster files, no labels in the paint.

## Level 13 — Rust Harbor

A beached warship breaker's yard at dusk. Rusted hulls, dock cranes, container
stacks, pier pilings over dark water. Palette: dusk blue `#1b2b4a`, burnt
orange `#ff9a4a`, rust `#6e3a22`, sand `#e8d8b0`, dark water `#0a1626`.

- Sky: gradient plus low sun disc plus cloud bands.
- Far: beached hull silhouettes, irregular spacing.
- Mid: dock crane frames (diagonals) plus container stacks.
- Near: pier pilings plus water shimmer.
- Deck: rust steel with a pale sand walk edge. Prop: dock lamp post (decor).

## Level 14 — Ember Foundry

A smelter plant interior. Furnace wall with crucible mouths, ladle crane rails,
melt channels below catwalks. Palette: soot `#140d0a`, ember `#ff6a2a`,
molten `#ffd23c`, iron `#3a2c26`, warning green `#7dff9a`.

- Sky: dark gradient plus rising ember columns plus spark drift.
- Far: furnace wall with glowing crucible mouths.
- Mid: ladle crane rails with hanging ladles (diagonals).
- Near: melt channel flow plus catwalk grate edge.
- Deck: iron plates with ember seams. Prop: vent stack with ember glow (decor).

## Level 15 — Skyhook Anchor

The orbital tether ground station at night. The ribbon climbs off-screen,
gantry towers flank the anchor pylon, service arms reach over the pad.
Palette: night `#060b18`, tether cyan `#7df9ff`, pad alloy `#39435a`,
signal amber `#ffb15a`, alarm red `#ff4a3c`.

- Sky: gradient plus climbing tether ribbon plus climber lights.
- Far: gantry tower frames, irregular heights.
- Mid: anchor pylon landmark plus service arms.
- Near: launch pad slab edge plus pipe runs.
- Deck: pad alloy with cyan seam. Prop: tether clamp post (decor).

Each candidate ships a background layout, one solid deck, one decor prop, and
a 1x-on-black preview with a seam strip. Composition devices per candidate go
in the manifest `spec`.
