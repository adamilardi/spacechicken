# Wicket 200 — level art brief (id 26)

Theme: night cricket stadium under floodlights (Rashid Khan's 200th T20I wicket, Sharjah).
Palette: top #05070f, mid #101a33, bottom #232f45, lamp wash #ffd75a, fence #0a0f1e.
World: 9000 x 900. Chicken stands on reused deck tops; background carries no collision.

## Directions (silhouette + rhythm, not palettes)

A. Floodlight bowl (SURVIVOR): asymmetric floodlight wash upper-right, even stands band far, one pylon-head landmark mid, picket boundary fence near. Landmark: floodlight pylon head. Set piece: sightscreen wall worn by the signature corridor.
B. Stands canyon (KILLED): enclosed tiered-stands canyon with pavilion clock tower — reads pagoda, not Sharjah cricket, and the canyon crowds the playfield.
C. Light-rig diagonals (KILLED): crossing truss diagonals with blank scoreboard slab — symmetric X centers dead space; weaker landmark than the pylon.
